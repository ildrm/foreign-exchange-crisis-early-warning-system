15

Limitations

Known blind spots, point-in-time constraints and model-domain caveats are part of the result, not footnotes to it.

  1. Every numerical value and historical event in this example is synthetic.
  2. No empirical event labels, training, final test, calibration, or alert-threshold validation were used.
  3. Reconstructed point-in-time status is not a genuine real-time backtest.
  4. Parallel-rate and option evidence are unavailable or not applicable in the fixture.
  5. Hazards are dependent; no overall any-crisis probability is calculated.
  6. Predictive contributors are associations and are not causal findings.

FX-CPM research report. Probabilistic early-warning signals are not declarations, causal findings, trading advice, or substitutes for expert country analysis.
